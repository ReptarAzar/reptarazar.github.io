What I’m focused on now

Adobe

Lead design across Adobe’s next-generation of video tools, with a focus on democratizing professional creative tools and AI-powered tools.

This past year, I helped ship: Premiere iOS, Firefly web video editor, Firefly native app, and a partnership with YouTube Shorts. Previously at Adobe, I led design of Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Acrobat web.

My role connects vision and execution and I care deeply about building durable and high-performing design teams and cultivating an open and inclusive creative culture.

Gallery House

Gallery House is a salon-style art space hosting intimate exhibitions, performances, and cultural gatherings for emerging artists and experimental work. We believe, in a post-covid culture where third spaces have vanished, the internet deepens isolation, and artists face pressure to monetize everything, in-person creative community, experimentation, and parties are radical, urgent forms of mutual aid. It began as a living-room gallery in San Francisco, California, in 2021, in response to the global pandemic and social distancing.
