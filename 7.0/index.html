What I’m focused on now

Gallery House

Gallery House is an artist-led exhibition space shaped by domestic scale, intimacy, and experimentation. It began as a living-room gallery in Brooklyn. The program centers emerging and mid-career artists working across painting, sculpture, installation, sound, photography, and performance. Exhibitions are often site-responsive, shaped by the constraints of lived-in spaces, and developed in close dialogue with artists, allowing risk, conversation, and forms of presentation that wouldn’t be welcome in a conventional white cube.

Openings function more like a gathering than the sterile spectacle of a traditional one. Sales matter, but they are not the primary motivation. The priority is long-term relationships with artists, careful placement of work, and audiences built through trust rather than scale. As the gallery grows beyond a single residence, it remains committed to these values.

This is where my interests in art, community, and responsibility come together, and where I’m actively investing creative attention. @galleryhouse.nyc

Work

Adobe

Lead design across Adobe’s next-generation of video tools, with a focus on democratizing professional creative tools, AI-native workflows, and craft-centric experiences.

This past year, I helped ship: Premiere iOS, Firefly web video editor, Firefly native app, and a partnership with YouTube Shorts. Previously at Adobe, I led design of Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Acrobat web.

My role connects vision and execution: naming direction early, creating clear strategic context, protecting quality under pressure, supporting teams through delivery, and taking products from zero to industry-leading. I care deeply about building durable and high-performing design teams and cultivating an open and inclusive creative culture. My team's principles: Be growth minded, sweat the details, and have fun doing it.
