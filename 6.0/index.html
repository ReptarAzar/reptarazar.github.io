Hi, I'm Chris. I'm an artist and design leader in New York. My focus is building tools for creativity and well-being.

Currently, I lead the design team for emerging video products at Adobe.

I'm passionate about building growth-minded design teams, creating strategic context, cultivating an open and inclusive creative culture, driving zero to industry leading products, sweating the details, and having fun doing it.

At Adobe, I’ve led the design of Acrobat on web, several new workflows in Premiere Pro and After Effects, and the development of new video product lines including Premiere on mobile.

Previously, I was a product designer at the Washington Post Innovation Lab and co-founded Little Tinker, a software and hardware company.

Outside of work, I’m a volunteer meditation facilitator with a local Buddhist sangha, part-time art student at Pratt Institute, and have run over 20 marathons.
