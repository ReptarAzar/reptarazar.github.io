About Gallery House

Gallery House is a salon-style art space hosting intimate exhibitions, performances, and cultural gatherings for emerging artists and experimental work. We believe, in a post-covid culture where third spaces have vanished, the internet deepens isolation, and artists face pressure to monetize everything, in-person creative community, experimentation, and parties are radical, urgent forms of mutual aid. It began as a living-room gallery in San Francisco, California, in 2021, in response to the global pandemic and social distancing.

Gallery House presents artists working across painting, sculpture, installation, sound, photography, and time-based media. We are drawn to artists whose work is in experimental stages, where process is visible in the work. The program emphasizes long-term relationships over one-off shows. Exhibitions are developed in close dialogue with artists and often remain flexible through installation and duration. Conversation, connection, and offline presence are part of the work. The priority is care: for artists, for context, for dialogue and connection, and for the work.

As Gallery House remains grounded in the values it was built on: intimacy and experimentation. If you are an artist, curator, or neighbor interested in contributing, please reach out.
